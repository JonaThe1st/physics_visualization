Magnetization Reversal (Stoner–Wohlfarth)

Single-domain particle, uniaxial anisotropy, easy axis as reference. Energy ε(θ) = K·sin²θ − 2h·cos(θ−θH), reduced field h = H/HK. The magnetization tracks the local energy minimum and jumps when its well disappears. Stronger K ⇒ larger switching field ⇒ open hysteresis; field below hc ⇒ reversible rotation. The astroid plots the switching-field envelope in the H∥–H⊥ plane.

hc = field where M·Ĥ crosses zero. θH=0°: rectangular loop, hc=K. Tilted (≤~45°): hc=K(cos⅔θ+sin⅔θ)−3/2. Steeper θ: M rotates before flipping, hc→0. θH=90°: reversible, no loop. Astroid point: green = saturated (unique minimum), red = inside, metastable (hysteresis).
