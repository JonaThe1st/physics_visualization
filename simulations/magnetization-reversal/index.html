Magnetization Reversal (Stoner–Wohlfarth)

Single-domain particle, uniaxial anisotropy, easy axis as reference. Energy ε(θ) = K·sin²θ − 2h·cos(θ−θH) with reduced field h = H/HK. The magnetization tracks the local energy minimum and jumps when its well disappears. Stronger K ⇒ larger switching field ⇒ open hysteresis; field much weaker/hmax below hc ⇒ reversible rotation.

hc = field where M·Ĥ crosses zero. θH=0°: rectangular loop, hc=K (abrupt flip). Tilted 0°–~45°: hc=K(cos⅔θ+sin⅔θ)−3/2 (SW switching field). Steeper θ: M rotates before flipping, hc drops toward 0. θH=90°: fully reversible rotation, no loop, hc→0.
