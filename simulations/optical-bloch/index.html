Model and conventions

The basis is |e⟩, |g⟩, with z = Pₑ − Pᵍ and ρₑᵍ = (x − iy)/2. The ground state is the south pole. Δ = ω₀ − ωdrive; Ω is a real drive along x. The rotating-wave and Markov approximations are used, with relaxation to the ground state.

Hℏ=12(Ωσx+Δσz)
x˙=−Δy−Γ2xy˙=Δx−Ωz−Γ2yz˙=Ωy−Γ1(z+1)
Γ2=Γ1/2+Γφ,Pe=(1+z)/2

The pulse duration is π/Ω or π/(2Ω), defined on resonance. The scan varies the free wait while keeping both pulses and all other parameters fixed. The dashed vertical line in each time plot marks the current playback time. Numerical curves use fourth-order Runge–Kutta integration.
