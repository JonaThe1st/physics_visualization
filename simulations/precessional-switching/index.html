Precessional Switching interactive (LLG)

Soft ferromagnet (thin film, plane x–y), initial M ∥ x̂. Field in the yz-plane at polar angle φ from ŷ (in-plane, optimal for switching) to ±ẑ. Undamped (α=0) precession about the effective field → energy-conserving orbit on the sphere. h = H₀/Ms, hK = HK/Ms; below the threshold hK/2 the orbit stays in the +x hemisphere (no switching), above it crosses −x. Reduced time τ = γMst. Drag to rotate.
