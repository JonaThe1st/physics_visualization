Model and formulas

Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert equation for the normalized magnetization m (|m| = 1):

dm/dt = −m × heff − α m × (m × heff), divided by (1 + α²)

heff = h − (Nxmx, Nymy, Nzmz) + hRF(t)

Fields are in units of Ms, time in units of 1/(γμ₀Ms). The equilibrium m₀ is found numerically from the energy density e = −h·m + ½ΣNimi².

The resonance frequency follows from the curvature of e around m₀ (Smit–Beljers): ω₀ = √(λ₁λ₂). The ratio of the orbit's semi-axes is √(λ₂/λ₁). For h ∥ z and m₀ ∥ z this gives the Kittel formula

ω₀ = √[(h + Nx − Nz)(h + Ny − Nz)]

The amplitude decay rate is Γ = α(λ₁+λ₂) / [2(1+α²)] and the damped frequency is ωd² = ω₀²/(1+α²) − Γ². Both hold for small deflections.
